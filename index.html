BobWare Clicker

Hold your bound key or button, it clicks. Let go, it stops. Each profile keeps its own keybind and timing config, so different buttons can run completely different click styles at the same time.

  • Multiple profiles — bind different keys to different click styles, switch by just picking up the other button.
  • Presets — Slow, Normal, Fast, Turbo. Pick one, tweak the numbers if you want, done.
  • Humanized timing — CPS and hold time drift on a random walk instead of repeating like a machine.
  • Startup ramp — about half your holds ease into full speed instead of snapping to it instantly.
  • Reaction delay — a randomized beat before the first click, like an actual reaction.
