Featured app

BobWare Clicker

Hold a bound key or button to click, release to stop. Every profile carries its own keybind and timing config, so you can bind different buttons to different click styles — Mouse5 for fast, F6 for slow — and run them independently, even at the same time.

  • Multiple profiles — add, copy, and delete keybind profiles, each with its own trigger.
  • Presets — Slow, Normal, Fast, and Turbo templates fill in CPS, hold time, and delay in one click.
  • Random-walk timing — CPS and hold time drift within a range instead of jumping randomly, mimicking a real click rhythm.
  • Startup ramp — about half the time, a hold starts slightly slower and speeds up, like it takes a moment to spin up.
  • Reaction delay — a randomized start delay before the first click.
